# What Happened to This Image?

If you clicked on a broken image and ended up here, you've just experienced a phenomenon known as **[Bit Rot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_degradation)** (also called data degradation or link rot).

Over the decades that this website has been online, it has moved across several different hosting providers, content management systems (like WordPress), and storage formats. During those transitions, some files were corrupted, lost, or simply failed to make the journey.

The image you were trying to view is unfortunately one of those casualties. It has succumbed to the slow, inevitable decay of the digital world.
