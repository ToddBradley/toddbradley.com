The week of Thanksgiving 2004, the Bradley family is all going
to Mexico!  This page is all about that trip.

[Here is the beach house](<http://www.locogringo.com/akumal/cpc.htm?name=Casa> Playa del Caribe) my parents have rented for all of us for the week.
And [here is an aerial
photo of the beach](http://www.locogringo.com/maps/akumal-s.html).  We're renting the Casa Playa del Caribe house.
We should be able to walk right out the back door and go swimming in
the South Akumal Bay.

Beth and I are taking this opportunity to spur us to finally
learn how to scuba dive.  We're going to take a class from
[Flatirons Scuba](http://www.flatironsscuba.com/) the weekend of
10/16 and 10/17.  After taking the
basic course at Front Range Community College, we will be ready to do what's
called an "open water referral."  That means that our instructor in
Colorado will write a letter of referral that we take with us to Mexico.
There, we will complete our training with 4 open water dives over 2 days, and
get our PADI open water certification.  We'll do that through the
[Akumal Dive Shop](http://www.akumal.com/).

We may dine
[here](http://www.akumalinfo.com/la_buena_vida.html) one night, at La
Buena Vida.

Saturday November 20 - fly to Cancun on United 1098, rent a car
from Executive, drive to our
beach house called Casa Playa del Caribe in South Akumal

Sunday November 21 - 9:00 AM open water diving lessons

Monday November 22 - tour Tulum ruins, snorkel in Akumal Bay,
relax

Tuesday November 23 - more open water diving lessons, hopefully
culminating in PADI Open Water Diver certification

Wednesday November 24 - relax on the beach?

Thursday November 25 - Thanksgiving, maybe go diving and then
have a Mayan feast

Friday November 26 - more diving or snorkeling

Saturday November 27 - pack our bags, drive back to Cancun, and
fly home on United 1099

Does our medical insurance cover us in Mexico?  Yes, for
physician visit, urgent care facility, or emergency room - but NOT for
recompression chamber.  That's why we became members of DAN, the Divers'
Action Network.

What would we do in case of other medical injury down there?  Pay for it out of
pocket, get receipt, and submit a claim later.
