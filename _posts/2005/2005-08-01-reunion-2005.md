This page is all about the 2005 Bradley Family Reunion!

**Dates**:  July 8 through July 11, 2005

**Location**:  Buena Vista, Colorado (actually Johnson
Village)

**Lodging:**

> [A Bed on the
> Arkansas B&B](http://www.bedonthearkansas.com/index.htm) - we will have all 4 bedrooms reserved, giving us exclusive use
> of the kitchen, hot tub, living room, BBQ grills, deck, etc.
>
> - Arkansas Room - reserved by Deb
> - The Cozy Nest - reserved by Todd
> - The Princeton Room - reserved by Betty
> - The Trout Creek Room - reserved by Matt
>
> [Buena Vista KOA](http://www.buenavistakoa.com) -
> This is the nicest campground in the area, for those who prefer staying in a
> camper.  Also, they have some tiny 1-room cabins without kitchens or
> bathrooms, which is an option.
>
> Other lodging options:
> [Adobe Inn B&B](http://www.bbonline.com/co/adobe/),
> [Super 8 Motel](http://www.super8bv.com/)

**Schedule of Activities:**

(see also <http://www.buenavistacolorado.org/calendar.asp#Jul05>)

> [Collegiate
> Peaks Stampede](http://www.collegiatepeaksstampede.com/) in Buena Vista

>
> - breakfast at the house unless the pancake breakfast starts
>   early enough

>   Please arrive at AVA Outpost by 9:15 AM.  We have reservations for:
>
>   - Todd
>   - Beth
>   - Matt
>   - Rick
>   - Jess
>   - Dawn
>
> Non-Adventure Track
>
> - pancake breakfast in Buena Vista's Columbine Park, followed by
>   the Western Parade at 10 AM
> - [antiques shopping](http://www.salidachamber.org/bizdirectory/viewsubtopic.php?SubTopicID=4&TopicID=16) and [gallery hopping](http://salidaartwalk.org/)
>   in Salida
> - fishing in the Arkansas River
> - visit [Mountain Spirit Winery](http://www.mountainspiritwinery.com/news.htm)
>
> 6:00 PM BBQ dinner at the house
>
> - everyone bring their own grillables
> - Betty is bringing lots of hamburger buns and condiments
> - Todd is bringing baked beans and whatever else he thinks of
>
> games and stuff
>
> hot tub

## Sunday July 10

> Adventure Track
>
> - very early breakfast on your own
> - climb
>   [Mt. Princeton](http://www.localhikes.com/HikeData.asp?DispType=0&ActiveHike=2&GetHikesStateID=1&ID=5640) (elevation: 14,197 feet)
> - soak and possibly lunch at [Mt.
>   Princeton Hot Springs](http://www.mtprinceton.com/index.htm)
>
> Non-Adventure Track
>
> - hike in the
>   [Collegiate
>   Peaks Wilderness](http://www.altrec.com/published/climb/rockymountain/collegiatepeakswilderness/)
> - soak in the [Cottonwood Hot Springs](http://cottonwood-hot-springs.com/) (our personal favorite) or [Salida Hot
>   Springs](http://www.salidapool.com/)
> - fishing or whatever else people want to do
>
> 6:30 PM dinner at the Buffalo Bar & Grill or Casa del Sol
