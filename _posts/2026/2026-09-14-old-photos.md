A few months ago Mom cleared out her life-long collection of photos. My brothers and I each got a care package of some really old photos of ourselves and also some other people. This weekend, I finished a project to scan all of mine and touch them up a little. They'll be archived along with the rest of my Photos library. So now it's time to subject you to some of them!

Here I am at age 1/2 (4 months), 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, 16, and 17. You can probably guess which of these were school photos. It's funny how I went from having straight blond hair at age 8 to having brown curly hair at age 10. Then, in my teens I fully embraced the _Flock of Seagulls_ / _Thomas Dolby_ influence. I knew from looking at my dad that I was going to start losing my hair soon, so I tried to make the most of it while I could.
