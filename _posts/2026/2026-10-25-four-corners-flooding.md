I don't know if you've read about all the flooding in the Four Corners area, but it's been crazy. Every river in the area is maxed out, even more than the crazy amount of flooding last year. I guess this is climate change.

Here's a photo from an area in Bears Ears National Monument. Earlier this fall, I went here to try to go on a hike, but was turned away due to the creekbed having too much water in it. That was when I took this first photo.

Then last week I saw this photo someone from the [Bears Ears Partnership](https://bearsearspartnership.org) took of the same place. Maybe you'll notice the difference between the September photo and the October photo.
