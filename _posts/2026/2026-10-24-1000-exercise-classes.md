I overslept this morning, and woke up with a headache. This made me grumpy, so much I almost skipped going to my exercise class. But I rallied and walked to the studio at 10:00 AM.

When I checked in for Pilates the person at the front desk pointed something out that was a big surprise - to both of us. Today was my 1000th Pilates class at this studio!

Wow! I've been exercising there for about 13 years, but the first few years were when the studio had a different owner. The new owner bought it and renamed it [Axis Pilates](https://axispilates.com) in 2017. So now, nine years later, I hit 1000. I guess that averages to about 2.2 classes per week. I don't think I want to do the math of how much money I've spent there. But hey, it's a good antidote for sitting. And for better or worse, I chose a career that requires lots of that. So it's been very good for me.
