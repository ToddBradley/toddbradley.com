For about ten years, I posted photos I took around Denver to Tumblr. Tumblr has had some ups and downs since then, but I don't think it ever quite achieved its potential. So today I moved all 39 of my posts here, and I'm shutting down the Tumblr blog.

You can see all the old Tumblr posts in the [Tumblr category](/categories/tumblr/). They're just photos, no interpretation. I was just trying to document everyday stuff that happens in and around Capitol Hill.
