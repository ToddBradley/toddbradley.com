Reading the New York Times this morning, I saw a quote from French President Macron about US President Trump.

It reminded me of a line from Broadway musical _Hamilton_. I have no idea if Aaron Burr really said this - probably not - but the sentiment is parallel.

[Emmanuel Macron 2026](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/02/world/middleeast/macron-trump-nato.html): "When we're serious, we don't say the opposite of what we said the day before every day, and maybe one shouldn't speak every day."

[Aaron Burr 1776 (fictional)](https://themusicallyrics.com/h/351-hamilton-the-musical-lyrics/3705-aaron-burr-sir-lyrics.html): "Let me offer you some free advice. Talk less. Smile more."
