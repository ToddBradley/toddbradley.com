**ATI Radeon 9000 64M DDR TVO:** This is a 64 megabyte AGP video card. 
Not sure what it's worth.  Works fine, but I have a faster card now.

**Linksys EtherFast Cable/DSL Router**: Plug one end into your cable modem
and the other end into your PC's ethernet port.  This provides NAT/firewall
and DHCP. I'm asking a very affordable $10 for this.

**Arriva 10Base-T 8 Port Micro Hub/Repeater**: 10 MB/s ethernet hub. 
Unfortunately, this doesn't do 100 MB/s ethernet. Worth maybe $5.

**Middle Atlantic studio furniture** - I have a main desk and 2 side
units.  The main desk fits in the corner of a room.  It has a main
work surface suitable for a computer keyboard and other desktop type stuff, two
built in racks of 6U each, and a middle riser for a computer monitor.  The
tops of the side racks on the main desk are perfectly situated for studio
monitors.  Then, the 2 side units both hold 12U of gear, with a top surface
that aligns with the main desk.

The middle piece is the MDV-CNR3 on this page:<http://www.middleatlantic.com/studio/mdv/mdesks.htm>

And the two side pieces are the MDV-R12's on this page:<http://www.middleatlantic.com/studio/mdv/mdvopt.htm>

The finish also looks like the photos on those web pages - graphite. I'd also
be including a drawer, which is D3 on this page:<http://www.middleatlantic.com/rackac/storage/drawers.htm>

...as well as some blank panels and some vent panels.

I paid $1200 for this setup when it was new, 4 years ago, and have been
careful to not make a single scratch on any of the surfaces.  It looks just
as good as when I bought it.  Due to the weight, I'm only interested in
selling this to someone who can come pick it up at my house in Broomfield,
Colorado.  I'll disassemble it and help you load it into a truck.
