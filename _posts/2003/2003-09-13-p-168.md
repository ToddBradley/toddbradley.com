##

Here’s my favorite deep thought by Jack Handey:

I think a good gift for the president would be a chocolate revolver, and since he’s so busy, you’d probably have to run up real quick and hand it to him.

In other news, we’re having a garage sale today. And the weather is crappy. It’s cold and drizzly, and not too many people are coming by to buy stuff.
