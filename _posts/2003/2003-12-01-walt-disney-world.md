Beth and I took a long weekend to visit Orlando, Florida.  The dates
were January 16 through 19, 2003, and the event was the marriage vow renewal of
John and Mary Hollis.  Here are my photos.

### Thursday, January 16, 2003

After a long travel by car, airplane, and then bus, we checked into Disney's
All Star Music hotel in Orlando.  The hotel is basically Disney's
equivalent of Motel 6 - short on amenities, but with a price to match.

Here's our room.
